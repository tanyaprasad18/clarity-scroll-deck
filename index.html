03 / The problemClarity / IBM
The gap

Impact is real. The evidence is fragmented.

Performance conversations depend on recall because the work that matters is scattered across the tools where it happens.

01

Review preparation

Review preparation becomes a search through a year of tickets, meetings, messages, documents, and delivery work.

02

Leader blind spots

Promotion, recognition, and resourcing decisions can be made with an incomplete view of contribution.

03

Growth ambiguity

Employees struggle to connect today's work to tomorrow's role, the skills they need, and the goals that matter.

Clarity replaces year-end reconstruction with a continuously built career story.
